Creation of online stores. Design & Front End Development.

To begin with, you need to send me a technical specification (terms of reference) for a future online store (it is possible in a free style) and, if available, corporate identity elements (logo, letterhead, etc.). After that, we determine the total amount of work, the final price and terms.

"Of the three things: fast, good and cheap, you can choose any two."

Important

During the layout of the online store, optimization for search engines will also be carried out. Because I think this is not unimportant, and this should happen precisely at the design programming stages.

Optimization includes working with internal factors - bringing markup in line with standards, improving its quality.

If the layout of the pages of the online store has oversights, the robot will not be able to fully and efficiently collect information.

I will also not ignore: the stylistic design of the text and its distribution over the markup (headings, bold, title, alt ...), improving the structure and navigation, the correct use of internal links, working on htaccess and robots.

Bonus

After the completion of the work, you will receive a good textbook "Website promotion and optimization. A short course" for free. This course is intended for authors and site owners who want to take a closer look at search engine optimization and promotion of their resource.
